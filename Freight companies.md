A **multi-tenant SaaS platform** for freight forwarders. They log in, load their shipments, and an AI agent routes everything autonomously. Question: What need to load their shipments automatically? There should be two options? One is the when shipments get generated elsewhere it gets push into the plaform. These shipments should then be planned on schedule. Think through the scheduling part. When fowarder log in they can see shipments that need to be planned. At fixed times all shipments are batched planned. Forwarder operators can also trigger and plan for any subset of the shipments whenever they want. Think through how they might want to select subset of shipments to plan. A forwarder can have many clients shipping with them. They may want to plan to a specific location. Just do a deep dive and think through these uses cases. Batch planning has a benefit, you can find better solutions overall for the entire set of shipments. Individual shipment planning is myopic and greedy. The other thing is about planning and execution. Do you want to soft plan to enable replanning? So separated from plan to execution. At some point you cannot soft plan anymore and you have to firm up the plan. Think about this concept. Ideally, you want to delay planning as much as possible. But you have to firm up decision so you can execute.
